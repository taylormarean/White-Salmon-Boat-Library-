Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Next Chapter (“Next Chapter,” “we,” “us”), operated by Fetchlist, is a job-search assistant at nextchapterjobhunter.com. It finds jobs, writes applications in your own words, sends them from your Gmail when you approve, and keeps track of replies. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version: we use your information only to run your job search for you. We never sell it. We never use it for advertising. Next Chapter can send and read your email, but it cannot delete anything, and by default nothing is sent without your OK.

1. Information we collect

Your Google account

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. Helpers (family members or friends you invite) share only their name and email address. We never access a helper’s Gmail or calendar.

Gmail (job seekers only, with your permission)

Next Chapter asks Google for two Gmail permissions: read (gmail.readonly) and send (gmail.send). It cannot delete, archive, or change anything in your mailbox. We use them to:

  • Read incoming messages to find replies from employers and applicant-tracking systems, and match them to your applications.
  • Read resumes, cover letters, and emails you have sent before, so it can learn your work history and how you write (your “voice”).
  • Send application emails, follow-ups, and replies from your address, only as you approve them, or automatically if you turn on autopilot in Settings for specific kinds of messages. Replies to a real person always wait for your OK.

We store only what your job search needs, such as employer replies matched to your applications, the messages we send for you, and text from past resumes and writing samples. We do not keep a copy of your whole mailbox.

Google Calendar (with your permission)

We use Calendar access only to create events for interviews you’ve scheduled and to check your free/busy times so suggested interview times work for you. We do not read the details of your other events.

Things you give us

  • Documents you upload, such as resumes, cover letters, and writing samples.
  • Your answers and settings: job preferences, location, salary wishes, your 👍/👎 votes and the reasons you give, notes, edits to drafts, and feedback.
  • Optional phone number, if you choose to receive text messages.

Job and application data

Job listings we find for you, the fit scores and drafts we create, the applications you send, and the replies and status of each application.

Browser helper (optional)

If you install the Next Chapter browser helper, it reads the application form on a job page when you ask it to fill that form, so it can enter your details and answers. It never presses Submit.

Usage and billing records

Records of how much AI work was done for your account and what it cost, your credit balance, purchases, refunds, spending caps, and referral activity. We also keep basic technical logs (such as sign-in times and errors) to keep the service working and secure.

Payments and referral payouts

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we receive only a record of the purchase (amount, date, and status). If you join the referral program and want to be paid, you set up a payout account with Stripe Connect. Stripe collects the identity, bank, and tax information it needs directly; we only see whether your payout account is ready and the payouts made.

2. How we use AI

To analyze jobs and write for you, Next Chapter sends the relevant content (for example, a job posting, parts of your resume and career history, your writing samples, and an employer’s reply) to Anthropic’s Claude API. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, Anthropic does not use content sent through its API to train its models by default. Anthropic may keep API content for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its own policies.

AI can make mistakes. That’s why drafts come with a “Before you send” note and wait for your OK by default.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: find and score jobs, write and send applications, follow up, track replies, and help you prepare for interviews.
  • To learn your preferences from your votes, edits, and feedback, so results improve for you.
  • To send you system emails (like your morning summary and account notices) and optional texts.
  • To bill for usage, enforce spending caps, process refunds, and run the referral program.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use your Gmail data to build profiles for anyone else.

4. Google user data: Limited Use

Next Chapter’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, this means:

  • We use Gmail and Calendar data only to provide and improve the job-search features you can see in the app.
  • We transfer it to others only as needed to provide those features (for example, to Anthropic to write a reply), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with your notice.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • People at Next Chapter do not read your Gmail data unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it’s needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Next Chapter, and only what each one needs:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the app and stores its data.
  • Anthropic: the Claude AI that analyzes jobs and writes drafts.
  • Google: sign-in, Gmail, and Calendar, at your direction.
  • Stripe: card payments, refunds, and referral payouts (Stripe Connect).
  • Resend: delivers system emails, such as account notices.
  • Twilio: delivers optional text messages, if you turn them on.

Your applications are, of course, sent to the employers you choose. Helpers you invite can see your job search. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information).

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is open, so your job search keeps working. You can delete documents and lessons in the app at any time.

To delete your account and your data, email support@nextchapterjobhunter.com from the address you sign in with. We will delete your account data, including your stored Gmail connection, within 30 days. We may keep limited billing and payout records where the law requires (for example, for taxes), and copies in backups may last a short time before they are overwritten.

You can also disconnect Next Chapter from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once you do, we can no longer read or send your email.

7. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Your Gmail access token is stored encrypted at rest.
  • Next Chapter requests the narrowest Gmail permissions that make it work, with no ability to delete mail.
  • Access to production data is limited to what’s needed to run and support the service.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

8. Your choices

  • Review and change your settings, documents, and autopilot options in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing support.
  • Turn off text messages or the morning email in Settings.
  • Disconnect Google access at any time (see above).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us and we’ll help.

9. Children

Next Chapter is for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has signed up, please contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we’ll update the date at the top and let you know by email or in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests? Email support@nextchapterjobhunter.com. A real person will get back to you.
