Next Chapter helper

How to use it

  1. Open a company's job application form.
  2. Click the green Next Chapter button at the top right of Chrome. (If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece and pin Next Chapter.)
  3. Press Fill this application.
  4. Check every box. Fill in the yellow ones yourself.
  5. Press the website's own Submit button yourself. The helper never submits for you.

Your privacy

The helper only runs when you press the button, and only on the page you're looking at. Your key is kept on this computer and is only sent to your own Next Chapter app.
