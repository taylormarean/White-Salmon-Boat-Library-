Self-serve since day one

Three steps to the put-in

The old paper checkout board went digital. Every safeguard is baked in, so the shed can stay open around the clock without anyone standing behind a counter.

1
✍️

Join online

Free forever. Sign the waiver once, tell us your experience level, and you're a member. New to whitewater? Rad — you'll just paddle with a buddy who knows the lines.

2
🔑

Get the code

Pick your gear in the app, agree to the river rules, and the shed code lands on your screen — like 384942. Grab your kit, scramble the lock, go.

3
🌊

Go paddle

Send it. Bring everything back drained and clean, mark it returned in the app, and be honest about damage — that honesty is what keeps the boats floating.

The river rules

🦺PFD on whenever you're on the water. Non-negotiable.
🚫Zero tolerance for drugs & alcohol with library gear. The river's enough.
👯New to the sport? You paddle with an experienced buddy. That's how everyone here learned.
🤝Report damage honestly. Nobody gets charged — the next paddler just deserves safe gear.

💸 Chip in

Every dollar goes to gaskets, welds, spray skirts, and shed rent. A donation the size of one shuttle beer keeps a boat on the water for a season.

🔧 Give time

Repair nights, orientation Tuesdays, inventory audits. Claim a volunteer shift in the app — no experience needed, mullet optional but encouraged.
