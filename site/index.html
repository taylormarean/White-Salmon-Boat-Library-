Free boats and gear for the White Salmon community

The White Salmon Boat Library is a local, donation-based non-profit in the heart of the White Salmon kayaking community. We lend quality whitewater gear to our members free of charge, so anyone can get on the river — financially risk-free.

Our mission

Everyone should have the opportunity to enjoy this sport

We aspire to promote whitewater kayaking, cultivate a love of the outdoors, and minimize the social and economic barriers to river sports. The library offers newcomers access to gear and boats — and a way to integrate into the community that comes with them.

Membership

Joining is free and takes a few minutes

Membership requires a signed waiver and a member profile — both are now completed online. Once you're signed up, you can check out gear any time.

1

Sign up online

Create your member profile and sign the liability waiver electronically. Please tell us honestly about your whitewater experience — it helps us keep everyone safe.

2

Come to an orientation

New Member Orientations are Tuesdays at 5:30pm at the library — we'll show you the facility, the gear, and how checkout works. Upcoming orientation dates are in the member app — reserve your spot after you sign up.

3

Check out gear, any time

The library is open 24/7 for members. Choose your gear online, complete the checkout, and you'll receive the door code right away. Rentals are 3 days (up to 9 for multi-day and out-of-town trips).

Library policies

A few rules keep the library running

All members agree to these when they join. Failure to comply can result in termination of membership.

🦺

Wear a PFDAll participants must wear a suitable personal flotation device while on the water.

🌊

Boat within your skill levelNo library equipment on Class V whitewater. If you're new to the sport, you must go with another person who can help you choose safe river sections and use the gear properly.

🚫

Zero tolerance for drugs and alcoholNo drug or alcohol use when on the river with library gear.

📋

Rental periodsThe rental period is 3 days. Longer rentals — up to the 9-day maximum — are reserved for multi-day runs and out-of-town trips, and must be noted at checkout. Gear leaving a 100-mile radius of the library? Tell us where it's going.

🔧

Care for the gearInspect your gear for defects before use — torn, ripped, or broken gear goes in the repair bin. Report any damage when you return, and put everything back in its correct location. Never share your access code.

💙

Respect everyoneThe library does not discriminate on the basis of race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, gender identity, or economic status — and expects the same of all members. Zero tolerance for any sexual, physical, emotional, or verbal harassment.

⚖️

Borrowing is a privilegeFailure to adhere to these policies will result in termination of library membership. A request for re-admittance can be made in the following year.
